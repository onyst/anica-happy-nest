House rules

A well-run site, kept that way.

Short version below — the reservation form covers the rest in detail.

Quiet hours from 9 PM

No speakers or loud music after 9 — courtesy to other campers.

Strays roam free

Dogs and cats live onsite. Don't approach or feed them — for everyone's safety.

Keep food covered

Store or cover meals before sleeping so strays can't get to them.

Trash goes in the bag provided

₱500 cleanup fee per group if the pitching area is left with loose trash.

Motorcycles stay parked

No in-and-out riding through the campsite — leave it in the parking area.

Early check-in is ₱200/pax

We keep this rare, out of courtesy to campers already booked in.

No check-out before dawn

To avoid disturbing campers still asleep.

Borrowed items, ₱100 hold

Raft, water containers, etc. — waived back if returned clean and intact.
